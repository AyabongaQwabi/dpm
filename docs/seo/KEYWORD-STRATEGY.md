# ServicePros Keyword Strategy

Date: 2026-08-05
Skills used: seo-keyword-strategist, seo-meta-optimizer, seo-content

## Strategic Position

ServicePros should not compete only as a generic "service provider directory". The strongest positioning combines:

- discovery: service providers in South Africa
- local intent: `{service} providers in {city}`
- transaction intent: book local services
- provider acquisition: get listed as a service provider
- category ownership: Directory and Provider Marketplace / DPM
- model differentiation: no pay-per-lead fees

## Source-Level Term Observations

The source scan shows strong repeated usage of `book`, `credits`, `verified`, `South Africa`, `pricing`, and `DPM`. This is good for brand/category consistency, but there are two risks:

- Customer acquisition pages may over-lean on broad `South Africa` and not enough on local service/category intent.
- Provider acquisition pages have strong model language, but `no pay-per-lead` should be more prominent in titles, H2s, and snippets.

Recommended keyword balance:

| Page type | Primary term | Secondary terms | Avoid overuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | service providers South Africa | trusted, verified, book local services | repeating "trusted" without proof |
| Search | find service providers | location, category, near me | indexing every query URL |
| Category | `{category} providers` | compare, reviews, services | generic copy shared across all categories |
| Category-city | `{category} providers in {city}` | local, verified, prices | boilerplate with only tokens swapped |
| Service detail | `{service title}` | package, price, provider, city | unsupported claims like "best" |
| Get listed | get listed as a service provider | no pay-per-lead, provider profile | vague "grow your business" without specifics |
| DPM | Directory and Provider Marketplace | DPM meaning, provider marketplace | any old expansion framing |

## Priority Page Briefs

### `/providers/category/[slug]/in/[location]`

Intent: high commercial local discovery.

Recommended content modules:

- 50-word direct answer: what this page lists.
- Provider list.
- "How to choose a {category} provider in {city}" checklist.
- "Typical services offered" list from actual services.
- "Verification signals to compare" trust box.
- Internal links to related nearby cities and adjacent categories.

### `/providers/service/[slug]`

Intent: service-specific comparison.

Recommended content modules:

- Direct answer for `{service} services in South Africa`.
- Service listing cards.
- Price range summary if real data supports it.
- Related service names.
- Top cities for the service.
- Link to `/services` and relevant category pages.

### `/get-listed`

Intent: provider acquisition.

Recommended H2 targets:

- `How to get listed on ServicePros`
- `What providers pay for`
- `No pay-per-lead fees`
- `What your provider profile includes`
- `How verification helps customers compare you`

### `/pricing`

Intent: credits, payments, buying objections.

Recommended H2 targets:

- `How ServicePros credits work`
- `What 1 credit is worth`
- `When credits are returned`
- `Credit packs`
- `Provider commission pricing`

## Internal Linking Rules

- Home should link to search, services, get-listed, DPM, verification, pricing, and top city/category hubs.
- Category pages should link to at least 6 city/category combinations where inventory exists.
- Location pages should link to at least 6 category/location combinations where inventory exists.
- Provider profiles should link back to their category, city, services, and verification page.
- Service detail pages should link to provider profile, service type page, category page, and pricing/refund where relevant.

## Measurement Plan

Track monthly:

- indexed pages by template
- GSC clicks/impressions for category-city patterns
- top queries for provider acquisition
- branded vs non-branded search split
- pages receiving AI/referral traffic
- conversion from organic sessions to booking or provider signup
