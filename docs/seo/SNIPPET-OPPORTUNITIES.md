## Recommended Snippet Blocks

Add these as visible copy near the top of the relevant pages, ideally after the H1 and intro. Keep paragraphs short enough that Google and AI answer engines can quote or summarize them cleanly.

### `/dpm`

Target query: `what is a DPM`

```txt
A DPM, or Directory and Provider Marketplace, is a platform where customers can discover service providers, compare profiles, view reviews, and book services. ServicePros is a DPM for South African local services, combining directory search with provider pages, service packages, booking, payments, and verification signals.
```

Target query: `directory vs marketplace`

```txt
A directory mainly helps people find listed businesses. A marketplace helps people transact with them. A Directory and Provider Marketplace combines both: searchable provider profiles for discovery, plus tools for pricing, booking, messaging, payments, and reviews.
```

### `/how-it-works`

Target query: `how does ServicePros work`

```txt
ServicePros works in four steps: search for a service or provider, compare profiles and reviews, book a service with credits, and review the provider after the work is complete. Providers manage listings, services, messages, bookings, and verification from their dashboard.
```

Target query: `how to book a provider on ServicePros`

```txt
To book a provider on ServicePros, choose a provider or service, review the package and price, sign in or create an account, pay with credits, and use your account dashboard to track messages, status updates, and reviews.
```

### `/pricing`

Target query: `how do ServicePros credits work`

```txt
ServicePros credits are used to pay for bookings. One credit equals R1. Customers buy credits, spend them on services, and receive credits back if an eligible booking is cancelled before work begins. Credits do not expire and can be topped up anytime.
```

Target query: `do ServicePros credits expire`

```txt
No. ServicePros credits do not expire. They stay in your account wallet until you use them on a booking.
```

### `/verification`

Target query: `what do ServicePros verification badges mean`

```txt
ServicePros verification badges show which details have been checked. Contact means the provider confirmed phone and email details. Google means the listing was matched to Google Places. CIPC means registered business details were checked. FICA means identity and address documents were submitted.
```

Add/keep a simple table:

| Badge | What it checks | What it does not guarantee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact | Phone and email reachability | Work quality |
| Google | Google Places business signal | Independent identity review |
| CIPC | Registered business details | Work quality |
| FICA | Identity and address documents | Work quality or outcome |

### `/get-listed`

Target query: `how to get listed as a service provider`

```txt
To get listed on ServicePros, create a provider account, complete your business profile, add services and prices, submit available verification details, and publish your listing. Once published, your profile can appear on category, city, and search pages.
```

Target query: `does ServicePros charge for leads`

```txt
ServicePros does not charge providers for enquiries or leads. Providers pay for their listing package and commission only on completed, paid bookings.
```

## Snippet Formatting Rules

- Put the answer immediately under an H2/H3 that mirrors the query.
- Use 40-60 words for definition snippets.
- Use numbered lists for process snippets.
- Use tables for comparison snippets.
- Avoid vague promotional copy in snippet blocks.
- Answer first, then add proof, examples, or CTA after.
